## Rule
Draft in a conversational register: contractions, short sentences, the word I
would actually say rather than its formal cousin.

## Detail

## Why
It reads better and it is faster to write, and most of what I produce is read
by people who know me.

## Story
Not an incident so much as a standing preference, written down the first time
a session guessed wrong about it twice in one afternoon.

## Install
Nothing to install.

Worth knowing what happens when this meets a shared standard. A team that
marks its own tone practice as blocking — *"anything we send a client is
formal"* — wins, and this one does not apply to that work. That is not a bug
in the ordering: a personal practice is about how I work, and a blocking team
practice is about what we ship. The tools report the refusal rather than
silently dropping either one, so it is visible which practice is in force and
why.
