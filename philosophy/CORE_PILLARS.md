# Core Pillars

Several things, not one, are what set this repo's approach apart from other AI harnesses.

- <a id="human-led"></a>**Human-Led for Human Collaboration.** A person sets the direction, decides what "right" looks like, and owns the outcome while the agents do the producing ([`people-manage-agents-execute`](OUR_PHILOSOPHY.md#people-manage-agents-execute)), and the point of it all is a *group* of people working a problem together through that same loop, not one person going faster alone ([`groups-not-individuals`](OUR_PHILOSOPHY.md#groups-not-individuals)).
- <a id="focus-on-whys-problems"></a>**Focus on Whys & Problems.** Every request opens with the problem and the reason behind it, not a prescribed fix, so the AI Assistant spends its effort actually solving the problem instead of executing a guess ([`explain-the-why`](OUR_PHILOSOPHY.md#explain-the-why), [`five-whys`](COMPANY_BUILDING_RULES.md#five-whys)) — [`understanding-problems`](HUMANS_AT_OUR_BEST.md#understanding-problems) is the human skill this pillar leans on.
- <a id="rules-emerge-person-approved"></a>**Rules emerge from precedents decided, person-approved.** The rules that govern how the work gets done are extracted automatically, as a side effect of doing the work, rather than written up separately afterward ([`protocols-generated-not-just-documented`](REASONS_WHY.md#protocols-generated-not-just-documented)).
- <a id="chat-is-the-entry-point"></a>**Chat & voice messages as the primary human entry point.** A person's way of touching any document or artifact here is to talk to AI Assistant and have AI Assistant make the change, never to open the file and edit it directly ([`ai-chat-as-intermediary`](COMPANY_BUILDING_RULES.md#ai-chat-as-intermediary); built into the system, [`chat-is-primary-interface`](AI_GOVERNANCE_TO_COCREATE.md#chat-is-primary-interface)).

None of them is new on its own; together, they make collaborative work look and feel different from anything a solo-developer-focused harness sets out to do.
