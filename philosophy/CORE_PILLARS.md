# Core Pillars

Several things, not one, are what set this repo's approach apart from other AI harnesses.

- <a id="human-led"></a>**Human-led.** A person sets the direction, decides what "right" looks like, and owns the outcome; the agents do the producing, but they never pick the goal or sign off on the result ([`people-manage-agents-execute`](OUR_PHILOSOPHY.md#people-manage-agents-execute)).
- <a id="collaboration-not-just-production"></a>**Humans collaborating with each other, not just producing.** Most "work better with AI" advice — coding assistants, integrations, personal productivity tools — makes one person faster at their own work; the point here is a *group* of people working a problem together, with chat and this repo as what connects them ([`groups-not-individuals`](OUR_PHILOSOPHY.md#groups-not-individuals)).
- <a id="rules-emerge-person-approved"></a>**Rules emerge from the work, person-approved.** The rules that govern how the work gets done are extracted automatically, as a side effect of doing the work, rather than written up separately afterward ([`protocols-generated-not-just-documented`](REASONS_WHY.md#protocols-generated-not-just-documented)).
- <a id="chat-is-the-entry-point"></a>**Chat & voice messages as the primary human entry point.** A person's way of touching any document or artifact here is to talk to AI Assistant and have AI Assistant make the change, never to open the file and edit it directly ([`ai-chat-as-intermediary`](COMPANY_BUILDING_RULES.md#ai-chat-as-intermediary)).

None of them is new on its own; together, they make collaborative work look and feel different from anything a solo-developer-focused harness sets out to do.
