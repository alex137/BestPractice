# Recipe: HUMANS_AT_OUR_BEST.md

- A new short list of "what humans are good at" showing up in some other
  document is the signal to add it here too — fold the new items into
  "The Four Aspects of the Human Genius" (dedupe against what's already
  there) and add this
  document's own parenthetical link at that new list's spot, matching the
  other two originals it already tracks.

- **Item labels are sentence case** — "The human spark.", "Sense of smell." —
  and the four subsection headings are title case. Retitling an item is free:
  citations use its slug, never its label, so a renamed entry keeps every
  link pointing at it
  ([backlinks.recipe.md](backlinks.recipe.md)). `questions` reads "The right
  questions." for this reason and keeps its original slug.
