### Implicit data caching
> By default, Google's published Gemini models cache Customer Data to
> reduce latency and accelerate responses. Implicit caching uses two storage
> tiers:
> In-memory caching: Enabled by default for supported Gemini
> models.
> Durable caching: Enabled by default for supported Gemini
> models. For the list of supported models, see
> Context caching overview.
#### In-memory data caching
> Implicit caching stores Customer Data (inputs, outputs, and derived data) in
> memory. In-memory cached data has the following properties:
> It's stored only in memory, not at rest.
> It's scoped to your project and is never served to another project.
> It's deleted no later than 24 hours after it's written.
> It's processed in the region that serves the request.
> It's used only to serve your requests faster, and is never used to train or
> fine-tune models.
#### Durable data caching
> On models that support durable caching, when in-memory cache capacity is
> exhausted, Google stores derived cache data (key-value representations computed
> from your inputs) in durable storage within the Agent Platform serving
> environment. Durable cached data has the following properties:
> It's stored at rest and encrypted with Google default encryption.
> It's scoped to your project and is never served to another project.
> It's deleted no later than 24 hours after it's written, and is often evicted
> sooner.
> It stays within the selected location and adheres to its Data Residency
> requirements when you use a regional or multi-regional endpoint. When you use
> the global endpoint, requests and their cached data can be served from any
> region.
> It's used only to serve your requests faster, and is never used to train or
> fine-tune models.
> Customer-managed encryption keys (CMEK) aren't supported for durable caching.
#### Control data caching
> If your organization's policy does not permit Customer Data to be stored at
> rest, you can
> disable durable caching on a per-project basis
> and maintain in-memory caching. Alternatively, you can disable caching
> entirely. To disable durable caching across your organization rather than on a
> project level, contact your account team.
